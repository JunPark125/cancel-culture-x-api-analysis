# Project overview

Junseok Park describes this work as undergraduate research assistance at UBC Sauder School of Business under Professor Jen Park. The repository presents technical research support for studying cancellation and boycotting language on X. It does not represent an official UBC publication or assert sole ownership of the study.

Two verified projects are distinguished throughout the code:

1. **Historical counts, 2015–2025.** Retrieve daily count buckets for two exact hashtags, aggregate them by month and year, and compare the monthly time series.
2. **2022 post collection.** Retrieve matching individual posts, preserve metadata in a raw schema, deduplicate by ID, prepare simplified exports, inspect quality, and compare collection coverage with a counts reference.

An earlier Balenciaga recent-search experiment is retained as a separate historical pilot. It fetches a single page, uses a fixed February 2026 window, and cleans URLs, mentions, and newlines. It is not a completed brand-level analysis.

X-specific model-labeling scripts and their outputs were also discovered. They contain project-specific classification instructions and remain local pending separate permission. Their existence does not establish validated classification accuracy. No model-labeling request was executed for this portfolio.

The portfolio excludes all original research data and original figures. A local audit records actual file sizes, hashes, row counts, and unresolved differences between source versions. The committed demonstration is synthetic and exists to make the engineering workflow reviewable without research-data access.
